# Content and CMS

**Usual disasters:** a draft published by mistake or by the wrong role; published content lost or
overwritten by an older version; scheduled content going live at the wrong time or not at all; private or
paid content visible to everyone; broken links and images after a change.

**Dirty paths:** two editors changing the same item; reverting to an older version; scheduling across time
zones; unpublishing content other items link to; uploading very large or oddly named files; renaming a
slug that is already shared.

**Where the owner looks:** the live site, the review queue, version history, scheduled list, traffic or
subscription reports.

**Questions:** Who can publish without review? What happens to links when content is moved or removed?
Which content is only for signed-in or paying readers? Is there a version history, and who can restore it?
