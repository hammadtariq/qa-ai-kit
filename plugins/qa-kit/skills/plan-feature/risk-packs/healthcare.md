# Healthcare

**Usual disasters:** a patient's record visible to someone without a reason to see it; the wrong patient's
record changed; double-booked appointments; a prescription or dose recorded wrongly; a change to a clinical
note with no trace; reminders sent to the wrong person or with private details.

**Dirty paths:** two patients with the same name and birth date; correcting a note after it is signed;
cancelling or moving an appointment at short notice; a clinician covering for another; time zones for
telehealth appointments.

**Where the owner looks:** the day's appointment list, the patient's record and its history, the access
log, messages sent to patients.

**Questions:** Who may see which parts of a record? Can a signed note change, or only be amended? Which
details may a reminder message contain? Which privacy rules apply (HIPAA, GDPR, a local law)?
