# Marketplace

**Usual disasters:** a seller paid too much or too little (commission, fees, refunds); a buyer charged
for an order the seller can't fulfil; payouts made before a refund window closes; one seller seeing or
changing another seller's listings or orders; fake or duplicate listings; a dispute decided without the
history to back it.

**Dirty paths:** a refund after the payout was sent; partial fulfilment of a multi-seller order; a seller
editing price or stock while an order is in checkout; a buyer and seller in different currencies; a seller
account suspended with orders in flight.

**Where the owner looks:** payout reports per seller, commission totals, the dispute queue, seller and
buyer order histories, platform revenue.

**Questions:** When exactly is a seller paid, and what happens to a refund after that? How is commission
calculated on discounts, delivery and refunds? Who decides disputes, and what must they be able to see?
