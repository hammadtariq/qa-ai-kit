# Booking

**Usual disasters:** the same slot booked twice (overbooking); a booking confirmed but never charged, or
charged but not confirmed; cancellations refunded wrongly against the policy; slots shown in the wrong time
zone; staff seeing or changing bookings they shouldn't; a customer not told about a change.

**Dirty paths:** two customers taking the last slot at once; booking across midnight or a daylight-saving
change; cancelling just before and just after the refund cut-off; rescheduling into a full slot; a no-show;
holding a slot during payment then abandoning it.

**Where the owner looks:** the availability calendar, the day's schedule for staff, confirmation and
reminder messages, refunds and no-show reports.

**Questions:** How long is a slot held during payment? What is the cancellation and refund policy, exactly
(hours, percentages)? Which time zone is a booking shown in, to the customer and to staff? Can staff
overbook on purpose?
